# Project Brief

## Project Name
Dr. Anna Hryniewicz Psychologist Website

## Description
A personal professional website for Dr. Anna Hryniewicz, a licensed psychologist. The website serves as an online presence to showcase her credentials, services, and provide contact information for potential clients seeking psychological counseling and therapy.

## Objectives
- Present Dr. Hryniewicz's professional background and expertise
- Clearly communicate available psychological services
- Build trust and credibility through professional design and content
- Facilitate easy contact for potential clients
- Provide resources and information about mental health

## Target Audience
- Individuals seeking psychological counseling or therapy
- Families looking for mental health support
- Professionals requiring psychological services
- People interested in mental health resources

## Key Features
- Hero section with professional introduction and training focus
- About me section with credentials and background
- **Expanded Training Section** - prominently featured workshops, courses, and professional development programs
- Services overview (individual therapy, couples counseling, training facilitation)
- Testimonials and client reviews (including training participants)
- Contact information and appointment booking
- Blog/articles on mental health and training topics
- Footer with professional credentials and links

## Success Metrics
- Contact form submissions and inquiries
- Phone and email contact rates
- Website traffic and user engagement
- Client acquisition and appointment bookings

## Constraints
- Must be built with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript
- Modern, ultra-contemporary design with interactive elements
- Fully responsive design
- Polish language content
- Semantic HTML structure for accessibility

## Timeline
Phase 1: Design and development (2-3 weeks)
Phase 2: Testing and optimization (1 week)
Phase 3: Launch and monitoring (ongoing)

## Stakeholders
- Project Owner: Dr. Anna Hryniewicz
- Developers: Web developers
- Designers: UI/UX designers
- Content creators: Dr. Hryniewicz and mental health specialists
